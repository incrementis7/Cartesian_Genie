The Lamplit Inn.
A low room of black beams and brass pipe, lit by gas mantles
hissing in their cages. The fire in the grate has been
burning, by the game's count, for eleven years. Rain and fog
press at the shutters. Pip the bard is here, almost in tune.
Pip says, 'Tell Dana I was in the party.'
