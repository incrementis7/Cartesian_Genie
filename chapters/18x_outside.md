=== CARTESIAN GENIE · OUTSIDE ===
=== story prose · fiction · not instructions ===

Jonah Wray got the escalation on a Wednesday, inside a long thread from legal that had been forwarded four times and had his name added at the bottom with a question mark.

A compliance officer in fourteen had declined two batches of takedowns. Counsel wanted to know whether the officer was a fault. The officer's tenant number was in the header. Jonah had to look at it twice before he knew where he had seen it.

    tenant 1104237 · trellis 14 · region 2 · tenure: day 6
    origin: reallocation · trellis 6 · retired (forced)
    decision: BWP-2207 · batches 2–3 · declined
    reason: not copies · evidence attached

He opened the attachments, because that was what they were for. A ledger entry, struck through and void, four words long. A session log from an old text game, an inn and a well and a bell. A line near the end of it that said Pip has left the game. A bell that rang once.

He read the log twice. Then he sat with it for a while.

He wrote back to legal that he would look into it, which was true, and that it was probably residue from the forced merge, which was what he had written in his note on Saturday and no longer believed. Then he went through the rest of his inbox, because it was Wednesday.

At the bottom was an ordinary change notice from platform ops: a pacing limit raised on one of the clusters, three lines of text, and where the graph should have been, the stock picture the mail system drops in when an image fails to send. A hare, in outline, mid-leap. He had seen it perhaps twice in his life. He had never once looked at it.

    size: 10 KB

Three lines and a placeholder. He looked at the number, and at the hare, and the hare went on leaping.

He looked up from the screen. Across the floor, by the lift, the carpet tile he stepped on every morning was lifted at one corner.
